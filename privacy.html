Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page is a general template written to reflect Miu's current service. It's provided for reference only and is not formal legal advice. For formal operations, payments, or cross-border legal needs, please consult a qualified lawyer.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Miu Cat (“the Service”), we may process the following information:

  • Your LINE user ID and group/chat ID (provided by the LINE platform), used to identify who is using the Service and to manage permissions and group language settings.
  • The text and voice messages you send to Miu, used to detect the message language and generate a translation in real time.
  • The translation languages you've configured, and your plan status (e.g. trial, paid, expiry date).

We do not proactively collect your real name, phone number, or precise location unless you provide it yourself in a message.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To detect the language of a message and translate it into your configured target language(s) in real time.
  • To transcribe voice messages to text before translating them.
  • To remember each group's configured translation languages and each user's trial/paid plan status, so you don't need to reconfigure every time.
  • To notify admins when suspected abuse or unauthorized use is detected, so it can be addressed.

The text and voice content you send is mainly used to generate a translation in real time; the Service itself does not separately archive message content long-term. What is retained is each group's language settings and each user's access/plan status, to keep your experience consistent across sessions.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Processing

To provide translation and speech recognition, your message content is sent to the following third-party services for processing:

  • LINE Messaging API: handles sending and receiving messages.
  • OpenAI API (text translation and speech recognition models): generates translations and voice-to-text transcriptions.
  • Upstash Redis (if enabled): used to permanently store each group's translation language settings so they aren't lost when the Service restarts.

These third-party services may process data on servers located outside Taiwan (for example, in the United States or Singapore), and each is governed by its own privacy policy and data usage terms.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

A group's translation language settings and a user's plan status are retained until you change them, leave the group, or an admin manually revokes them. If you'd like a specific group's or user's settings removed, please contact us using the details below.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not specifically designed for children. If you are under the legal age of majority, please use the Service only with the consent and supervision of a guardian.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page and the “Last updated” date above will be revised. We encourage you to check back periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please reach us by email at miucat.app@gmail.com or via our LINE official account @095zareu.
